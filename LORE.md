# BetterCopy Project Lore

A speculative timeline and narrative guiding the vision, community adoption, and long-term impact of BetterCopy.

## Timeline

* **Week 1 (The Video):** The video goes up. Split screen, portrait, 10,000 photos, no cuts. Explorer's bar is at 40% when yours finishes. The caption is the one you already wrote. It does 2.3M views in four days, and the top comment is *"why is this not just how windows works"* with 40k likes. The second comment is a guy saying just use FastCopy, and it has 200 likes, and nobody clicks it.
* **Week 2 (The Discussion):** It hits r/Windows, then HN. The HN thread is 600 comments and it is not about you — it's about Microsoft. It's about `explorer.exe` doing serial I/O in 2026, about the copy dialog's estimated-time algorithm, about how everyone has quietly given up. Somebody links the Raymond Chen post. Somebody else posts the FastCopy interview and says this guy has been telling us for twenty years. You are the occasion for the conversation, not the subject of it. That's the best possible position.
* **Week 4 (The Adoption):** 200,000 installs. The Rust community has adopted you. Someone has made a mac version. Someone has made a Linux version as a joke, because Linux already copies fine, and that joke gets more upvotes than your original post. Your GitHub has 18k stars and eleven issues about Norton flagging the binary.
* **Month 2 (The Emails):** The thing you didn't plan for: the emails. A DIT in Atlanta says he's been using it to pull camera cards and asks if you can write a checksum manifest. A guy who does storage migrations for county governments asks if there's a CLI. A game studio in Warsaw asks if they can deploy it to 300 machines and whether you have an MSI. You have a spreadsheet now. It has forty rows and every row is a business.
* **Month 4 (BetterCopy Pro):** You ship BetterCopy Pro. Not a faster version — the fast version stays free, forever, that was the promise and you keep it and people notice you kept it. Pro is verify-on-write, multi-destination, a report you can hand to a producer, an MSI, a CLI. $14/seat/year. The first 500 seats sell without a single sales call because the free tier already did the selling, on 300,000 machines, for four months.
* **Month 8 (The Contact):** A Microsoft PM emails you. It is a very friendly email. It says they've been "watching the discussion with interest." Nothing comes of it, but you screenshot it.
* **Year 2 (The Suite):** BetterCopy is a suite. It's the thing you install on a fresh Windows box before you install Chrome. It's in every "clean install checklist" video on YouTube. It has a philosophy that people can state out loud: *no admin, no injection, no residue, disappears when it's done*. That sentence is the brand. Twelve thousand paying seats. You are not rich, but you are free, and you are the person who did the thing everyone thought couldn't be done because they'd never tried.
* **Year 3 (The Validation):** Windows 12 ships with a parallel copy engine. They don't credit you. But you know. And so does everyone in that HN thread.
